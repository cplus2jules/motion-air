# Acknowledgements

Motion Air was built with **Joypad Air in mind** and is directly derived from
[Joypad Air](https://github.com/mindavidev/joypad-air), created by **David García
(mindavidev)**. Joypad Air provided the phone-to-Mac controller foundation,
including the web/Expo controllers, keyboard bridge, DSU/cemuhook integration,
and emulator setup tooling.

Motion Air is an independent continuation maintained by **Julian Salas
(cplus2jules)**, focused on a native iPhone controller, secure local pairing,
the Mac launch experience, and Just Dance and other motion games. The controller
mark continues the project's red/blue hardware identity.

The original MIT license and copyright notice remain in [LICENSE](LICENSE)
and [app/LICENSE](app/LICENSE). Original Git history and authorship are preserved.

Just Dance, Ryujinx, Dolphin, Cemu, and other product names identify the games
and software discussed in this project. Motion Air is an independent community
project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by their owners.
